## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a refined original logo for Leið, a music-making web app that turns public transit routes and station arrivals into musical notes. Deliver ONE clean horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent background, generous transparent margin, no presentation board or mockups. Symbol on left, wordmark on right. Symbol: a beautifully balanced, compact abstract lowercase l / route path made from a small number of bold precise strokes with a bend and two integrated station nodes; subtly evokes a step sequencer or melodic route without literal music-note clipart. Strong silhouette legible at favicon scale, flat vector-like geometry, disciplined negative space, professional transport-signage clarity and electronic-instrument character. Symbol colour signal lime #b6d94c. Wordmark colour cool white #eef0f3 for placement on dark app backgrounds. Exact text: "Leið" (capital L, lowercase e, lowercase i, lowercase Icelandic eth ð, Unicode U+00F0; NOT d, NOT đ; four letters). Custom-feeling modern grotesk lettering, medium-bold weight, excellent kerning, clear eth crossbar. Match the spirit of a night timetable and a serious DAW. Only this symbol and wordmark. No tagline, no extra text, no gradients, no shadows, no glow, no 3D, no hairlines, no enclosing app tile. Output crisp high-resolution logo artwork with actual alpha transparency.
