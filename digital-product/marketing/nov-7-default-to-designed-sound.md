# November 7 short: default sound to designed sound

Target: Saturday, November 7, 2026 at 18:00 Europe/Budapest (CET). Channels: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and X. Status: revised concept approved in direction by founder; recording, patch selection, final narration and video remain to be produced.

## Founder direction

Replace the single-note edit concept. Show the actual default sound of a lane when starting the app, then demonstrate what the user can achieve with a different instrument (Resonator or Macro), arpeggiator, Texture and effects. The outcome should make sound-design control audible.

## Core idea

One transport route supplies the source pattern. The listener hears its initial sound, the finished patch, then a short sequence showing how the patch was built. Opening overlay: **Default sound → Your sound**. Spoken opening candidate: **“Here's the default sound.”** Give the default a short musical phrase, then reveal the finished version immediately with **“And here's the same route.”**

This is a sound-design transformation, not a test of a single variable. Instrument, arpeggiator, Texture and FX may all change. Preserve the source route, stop/note edits, base harmony, loop and tempo so the source relationship is clear; arpeggiation can deliberately change audible rhythm and pitch order.

## Suggested 25–30 second edit

| Time | Picture and sound | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Actual lane at its genuine initial settings; play a short phrase | Default sound |
| 3–7 s | Immediate reveal of the completed patch, same phrase position | Your sound |
| 7–11 s | Show selecting the chosen instrument; hear this stage | Resonator OR Macro, whichever the actual patch uses |
| 11–15 s | Enable the actual arpeggiator and let its motion be heard | Add an arpeggiator |
| 15–19 s | Enable Texture, then the selected effects; close crops of real controls | Add Texture / Add effects |
| 19–25 s | Hear the finished sound with no narration | Same route. Your sound. |
| 25–28 s | Music continues with one invitation | Make your own · link in bio |

Timing follows the recorded phrase. The first finished-patch reveal should arrive by approximately three seconds. The middle is a compact demonstration, with controls and short labels doing most of the explaining. Keep clear listening space around both the before and after.

## Recording decisions

- Inspect the real initial lane state and capture it before editing. Enabling its playback is fine; do not replace its initial instrument or choose a deliberately weak patch and call that the default.
- A lane may initially be disabled. Label the initial sound after enabling and playing it accurately; do not imply that loading the page automatically produces this audio.
- Choose a route whose unedited pattern supports a convincing transformation. Berlin U4 is a candidate, not a fixed selection or a claim that the previously shared U4 bass patch is the page default.
- Choose Resonator or Macro for one coherent patch. Verify the chosen instrument is available in the actual recording surface; Macro availability has previously been flag-gated. Both are alternatives, not mandatory sequential swaps.
- Capture baseline, instrument-only, arp, Texture/FX and finished stages from the same working arrangement. Use actual UI and matching recorded audio. Keep source snapshots so the stages are reproducible.
- Match perceived playback level between default and finished excerpts. A louder after must not carry the whole transformation.
- Keep the music-only examples soloed to the demonstrated lane initially. Additional layers need explicit labels if later introduced.
- Identify the actual FX used once the patch exists. A label should describe an audible action rather than merely listing features.
