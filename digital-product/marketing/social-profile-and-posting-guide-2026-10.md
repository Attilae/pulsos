# Leið launch on Deettalabs: social profiles and publishing guide

Prepared **October 19, 2026** with the marketing skill. Audience: electronic musicians, curious producers, and people interested in the cities shown. Goal: earn a visit to `layth.space` and a real attempt to play the instrument. Constraints: solo founder, no existing audience, almost no promotion budget. This continues the [launch strategy](launch-strategy-2026-10.md).

**Updated October 20:** the founder already has Deettalabs accounts on all three platforms and plans additional audio plugins and products. Use those existing company accounts for the Leið launch. This replaces the earlier recommendation to create dedicated Leið accounts. The publishing mechanics and platform-specific CTAs still apply.

The copy and publishing choices below are recommendations for Leið, not promises of reach. Platform mechanics are sourced where verified. Instagram Help Center access was blocked during research; its exact current menu paths and feature availability need confirmation in the app. Readable Meta newsroom guidance was checked instead. No accounts or posts were changed.

## 2. Instagram profile and first Reel

### Profile

For the existing Deettalabs account, my recommendation is a **public professional Business account**. A Creator account would be a reasonable alternative for a personal musician account, but this profile represents the company and its products. This is an account-management choice, not a claim that one type gets better reach.

In the app, look under Profile → menu/settings → account type or professional tools → switch to professional → Business. Labels vary. Choose the closest honest software/app category, and hide the category label if it clutters the profile. A public profile lets the demonstration reach people outside the follower list; Meta describes this distinction in its [Reels overview](https://about.fb.com/news/2020/08/introducing-instagram-reels/).

In **Edit profile**, add this bio:

```text
Independent tools for making music.
Building Leið: city routes → music.
Play it in your browser ↓
```

Add the Instagram URL from section 6 in the profile's external-link field. Title the link **Play Leið** if a title field is offered. Open the public profile and test the link. Ordinary Reel captions are not a dependable clickable website placement; route the CTA to the profile link and confirm available options in your own account.

Later, pin the introduction Reel if the option is available. Add a “Start here” Story highlight once you have a short how-to and a working product link. These are optional profile improvements, not prerequisites for the first upload.

### Publish

1. Transfer the clean MP4 to your phone without exporting it through another social platform.
2. Tap Create/+ → Reel and choose the full file. Our existing master is vertical 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps, with 23 seconds of picture.
3. Keep the video's original music and narration. Do not add a second soundtrack over the musical demonstration.
4. Preview the whole video. It already has visible captions; avoid adding a second overlapping caption layer. Check that app controls do not hide `layth.space`.
5. Choose the cover. Instagram's cover picker may let you use the supplied PNG; check its profile-grid crop. Keep “Budapest became music” legible.
6. Paste the caption below. Check the AI disclosure control for the realistic generated narration; see section 5.
7. Select a public audience and profile/feed sharing where offered. Use the highest-quality upload setting if available. Publish when you can answer replies.
8. Share the Reel to your Story. If the Link sticker is available, attach the Instagram Story URL from section 6 and label it **Play your city**. Put it away from the map's musical action and subtitles.

Suggested caption for video 1:

```text
I turned Budapest's transport routes into music.

In Leið, routes become tracks and stops become notes. This arrangement uses trams 41 and 56, drums, and route-driven automation.

Play your city in your browser. Link in bio.

Real Leið footage and music. AI-generated narration.

#Budapest #GenerativeMusic #MusicProduction
```

An optional first comment can start a useful conversation:

```text
Which should I play next: Berlin or Helsinki?
```

Keep the product trial as the main caption CTA. The question is an optional conversation starter. Do not ask people to like, save, share, follow, comment, and visit the website in the same caption.

For account-specific advice, check Professional dashboard → **Best Practices** if available. Meta describes advice on creation, engagement, reach, monetization, and guidelines in this [official introduction](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/). Exact account and app availability still needs an in-app check.

## 3. TikTok profile and first post

### Profile

Use a **Business Account** for the existing Deettalabs account. TikTok recommends this when the primary goal is promoting a business. Its general music library is restricted for business accounts; our launch demonstration should use its own Leið audio anyway. [Account types](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/growing-your-audience/switching-to-a-creator-or-business-account)

Setup path: Profile → menu → Settings and privacy → Account → Switch to Business Account. Choose an accurate category and complete the ordinary profile fields. Business account selection and registered-business verification are different steps.

Use this bio, which remains useful even before a clickable website is available:

```text
Building music tools.
Try Leið: city routes → music.
layth.space
```

Check **Edit profile → Links/Add** for the website field. TikTok's specific link guide says a website can be added with **1,000 followers or a Registered Business Account**, and some linking features vary by location. Its broader account-type guide describes website links more generally. Follow the actual account's eligibility and verification flow; switching to Business alone is not a guarantee. Do not enter inaccurate registration information. [Website-link guide](https://support.tiktok.com/en/getting-started/setting-up-your-profile/linking-another-social-media-account)

If eligible, add the tagged TikTok profile URL from section 6. Otherwise retain the typed domain in the bio and on screen. If linking your Instagram or YouTube is offered, it is an optional fallback, with an additional step before the website.

### Publish

1. Tap + → Upload and select the clean MP4. Keep the full edit and original sound.
2. Preview the audio, captions, and visible website. Do not cover existing captions with native text or stickers.
3. Select a cover frame showing “Budapest became music.” A separate cover PNG is not required for this workflow.
4. Paste the caption below, using the correct CTA for your link eligibility.
5. Open **Content disclosure and ads**; it may be under More options. Enable commercial-content disclosure and choose **Your brand**, because the post promotes your own product. TikTok labels this “Promotional content”; third-party branded content uses a different selection. [Disclosure instructions](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service)
6. Turn on the AI-generated content disclosure for the realistic AI narration. See section 5.
7. If TikTok asks for Music Usage Confirmation for the original sound, confirm only if you have the necessary rights to the music, samples, and narration. Original sounds are permitted subject to those rights; TikTok's general-library licenses do not automatically cover commercial promotion. [Commercial music guidance](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/commercial-use-of-music-on-tiktok)
8. Check the public audience and comment settings, then publish. Leave boosting/Promote out of this organic launch workflow.

Suggested caption when there is **no clickable website field**:

```text
I turned Budapest's transport routes into music.
Routes become tracks. Stops become notes.
Try it in your browser: layth.space

Real Leið footage and music. AI-generated narration.

#Budapest #GenerativeMusic #MusicTech
```

When the website field is present and tested, replace the third line with:

```text
Play your city. Website link on my profile.
```

Do not promise a “link in bio” that the viewer cannot click. A plain domain is useful as something to type, but it will not preserve campaign tags.

### Publish

1. In YouTube Studio, choose **Create → Upload videos** and select the clean MP4. YouTube accepts square or vertical Shorts up to three minutes; this 23-second vertical file fits. [Upload guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12779649?hl=en)
2. Use the title and description below. A large list of tags is unnecessary for this launch workflow; use the city and the musical idea in ordinary language.
3. Set the audience accurately. This particular music-software demonstration is aimed at general/adult music makers, so the appropriate selection is **not made for kids**, assuming you have not changed its intended audience. This is different from whether children might happen to watch it.
4. Review the AI-use/altered-content disclosure control for this AI-narrated upload. See section 5. Keep any sponsorship answers accurate; do not invent a third-party paid partnership for a demonstration of your own product.
5. Run the upload checks and resolve any actual audio claim before release. Choose Public or schedule the release. Check that HD processing has completed before evaluating the picture.
6. For the Short's thumbnail, select the opening title frame **inside the YouTube mobile app**. YouTube documents frame selection there, rather than uploading a separate custom Shorts PNG through Studio. [Thumbnail instructions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10343433?hl=en)
7. Open the published channel on a phone and test the website link.

Title:

```text
I turned Budapest's transport routes into music
```

Description:

```text
Budapest's transport routes became this arrangement in Leið, a musical instrument you can play in your browser.

Routes become tracks. Stops become notes. This session uses trams 41 and 56, drums, and route-driven automation.

Try Leið using the website link on my channel: layth.space

Real Leið footage and music. AI-generated narration.

#Budapest #GenerativeMusic #MusicTech
```

Use **“website link on my channel”**, not “link in the description.” YouTube explicitly makes URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments non-clickable, while channel profile links are clickable. A pinned comment containing the domain does not bypass this. [Link rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en)

`#Shorts` is optional for our copy, not part of the filename, and not a substitute for a suitable video. The upload guide classifies the format through duration and aspect ratio. A related-video link is a useful later route to a real tutorial if available, but it points to YouTube content rather than directly to the product.

## 5. Audio, disclosure, and product claims

Keep the existing music. The sound is evidence of what Leið can make. Do not replace it with a popular song for reach, and do not add another platform's music and assume that license travels with a downloaded copy to the other platforms.

The current video uses a realistic **ElevenLabs-generated narrator**, with real application footage and music from the founder's session. Keep that distinction clear:

```text
Real Leið footage and music. AI-generated narration.
```

For this upload, use TikTok's AI-generated label and Instagram's applicable AI disclosure control. TikTok requires labeling realistic AI audio, and Meta states a disclosure requirement for realistic-sounding digitally created or altered audio. [TikTok guidance](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content), [Meta guidance](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/labeling-ai-generated-images-on-facebook-instagram-and-threads/)

For YouTube, my recommendation is to disclose this generated narration through its AI-use/altered-content setting as well, and keep the explanatory line in the description. Its policy distinguishes realistic or meaningful AI generation from minor assistance, and the examples are not exhaustive. This recommendation is not a claim that normal synth music, a screen recording, or AI-assisted script drafting by itself requires that label. Read the current upload wording when answering. [YouTube disclosure policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)

Commercial-content disclosure and AI disclosure are separate questions. On TikTok our post needs the own-brand disclosure as well as the narration disclosure. The written caption is context, not a replacement for a required native setting.

Describe the mechanism as **transport routes/data becoming music**. Do not call this “live arrivals” or “real-time buses making the beat”: live feeds are disabled in the supplied project instructions. Do not promise free unlimited exports, no signup, or support for every city. Those are separate product claims requiring a current check.

A shared profile URL identifies the platform, not the particular video that persuaded someone. Do not rotate the profile's `utm_content` for every new video: older videos can still send visitors. Typed-domain visits, stripped tags, cross-device returns, and blocked tracking remain incomplete attribution.

Record each upload's results at **48 hours and seven days**. Keep views, watch behavior/retention, shares or saves, profile activity, tagged site visits, and actual product-play attempts as separate observations. Do not present unjoined playback totals as conversions from a specific video.

After six core videos, use repeated signs of actual product interest to choose the next format. If views and profile visits rise without site visits, check the link and CTA. If site visits rise without playback, check the first-use experience. These are operating decision rules, not statistical proof of a channel's causal effect.

## 7. Reusable caption formula and launch routine

For later videos, write:

1. **Hook:** the city, route, or audible transformation.
2. **Explanation:** one or two sentences connecting what viewers hear to the real product.
3. **One primary CTA:** the exact next step and its real destination.
4. **Relevant disclosure:** when applicable to that video.
5. **A few relevant hashtags:** city, musical topic, and audience.

Our sample captions use three hashtags as an editorial choice, not an algorithmic optimum or platform maximum. The hashtag set should describe the actual post. Ordinary descriptive language matters more than filling the caption with generic `#fyp`/`#viral` tags. Neither hashtag counts nor a universal posting hour have been established as a reach guarantee for this account.

The existing plan starts on **Tuesday October 20**, then Thursday and Saturday. Use around **18:00 Budapest time** because you can be present to reply; this is a practical starting slot. Reuse the same clean master across platforms and adapt the caption and CTA. Do not delay a good musical demonstration to build a separate edit for every platform.

Before every post: listen on headphones and a phone speaker; check all text and cover crops in the native preview; test the public destination; check the applicable disclosure settings. After posting: check the public result, answer real questions, and note confusing parts for the next video. An optional question about the next supported city is useful conversation, not a substitute for inviting someone to play.
