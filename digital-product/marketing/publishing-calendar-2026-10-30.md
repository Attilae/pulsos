## Scheduling and review

This file is a publishing plan. **No new post has been scheduled or published in any platform by creating it.** When scheduling, verify the account and displayed timezone, use the prepared clean MP4 and platform copy, retain the existing disclosure treatment, and confirm the scheduled item in each platform. If a platform/account does not offer scheduling, use the same time as a manual publishing reminder. No paid scheduler is needed for this plan.

For TikTok, check the first video's **More insights / Analytics** for an explicit recommendation restriction and check system notifications. Investigate any specific notice; zero views alone does not identify the cause. Keep other platforms on their own publishing plan. [TikTok review and eligibility instructions](https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/account-and-user-safety/content-violations-and-bans), rechecked October 30. The October 29 screenshot established that this commercial post requires the TikTok mobile app for caption editing.

Review each upload around 48 hours and again at seven days. Record within-platform retention/engaged views where available, useful questions, profile or article visits, and separately observed site/playback activity. For articles, note article visits and available outbound preset-link clicks; do not claim click tracking exists without checking it. Exact untagged links and typed visits leave attribution gaps. Views, clicks and playback counts are not proof of incremental users or revenue.

Before reading results, commit to this decision: after the next three distinct shorts, use repeated viewer questions and retention drop-offs to choose the next topic and opening. A few views are not enough to rank platforms or identify an optimal posting hour. After two weeks, keep two shorts plus one article if it is sustainable; increase production only when fresh material and actual interest justify it.

Ownership: founder reviews music, publishes/schedules and replies; marketing supplies briefs/copy and interprets early observations cautiously. No product-code change, account change, external outreach or Reddit action is part of this calendar.
