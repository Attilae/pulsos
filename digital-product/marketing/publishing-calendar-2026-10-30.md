For articles, note article visits and available outbound preset-link clicks; do not claim click tracking exists without checking it. Exact untagged links and typed visits leave attribution gaps. A few views are not enough to rank platforms or identify an optimal posting hour. After two weeks, keep two shorts plus one article if it is sustainable; increase production only when fresh material and actual interest justify it.
