# Founder story: music + public transport → Leið

Prepared November 9, 2026. Review-stage concept and recording guide. A revised 30-second captioned direction-test MP4 was rendered November 11 using both music-making clips, all three transport clips, founder-at-work footage and a sequence of desktop product screenshots. Founder narration and additional footage remain pending; the video is not scheduled. The loop/bass feature remains a separate, paused production thread. This founder story's publication date is not assigned.

## Creative diagnosis

B: the founder supplied a specific personal premise and physical opening, a drum fill in a rehearsal room followed by Budapest transport footage. Preserve that real voice and sequence. Tighten the connective text and give the product one clear explanation plus an audible result. Avoid inventing an emotional backstory, years of development, a life-changing commute or claims about who uses it.

Audience: musicians/producers and people curious about music and transport. Goal: understand why the founder built Leið and visit layth.space to hear/play it. The product is a self-serve browser music tool. This is the personal introduction to the feature demos, with founder-owned footage and actual Leið audio as proof. Creative choices below are editorial judgment, not measured reach predictions.

## Recommended script

1. I really like making music.
2. And I really like public transport.
3. So I built Leið.
4. It turns transport routes into musical patterns.
5. The stops become notes. I choose the instruments and shape the track.
6. Here's one I made with Budapest.
7. [Music only: let the actual Leið track play for about four seconds.]
8. You can try it at layth dot space.

Speak Leið as “layth”. Say the URL naturally as “layth dot space”; display layth.space. About 25–30 seconds including a brief drum fill and the music-only proof. Final duration follows the founder's delivery and the musical phrase, not a fixed voice speed.

The original “That's why I created Leið” also works. “So I built Leið” is the preferred shorter spoken bridge. Keep “really like” if that sounds like the founder; changing both lines to “love” is optional and should reflect how they actually speak.

## Opening / alternatives considered

Keep spoken “I really like making music.” The drum performance makes it specific, but the sentence alone does not explain the product yet. Add the first-frame text “I turned Budapest's routes into music.” so a viewer understands what they are about to hear.

Alternate opening if the founder prefers a faster statement: “I make music. I also love public transport.” Not the default, since the supplied wording has a more natural personal rhythm.

Alternate curiosity opening for a separate edit: “I wanted to hear what Budapest's routes could play.” Use only if the founder confirms that describes their original motivation; it must not invent the origin story.

## Production direction

Use the existing Remotion project for the montage, captions and final assembly. Use real product screenshots, framed as large desktop views in the vertical edit. Avoid fabricated settings or presenting static screenshots as live synchronized playback. The matching Budapest track provides the audible proof while the screenshots hold on the route map and DAW controls.

Keep the video personal: rehearsal-room footage, real transport clips, the founder's own speech and a real founder-made arrangement. Add plain speech captions. Keep important faces/captions/URL away from screen edges and bottom platform controls; final safe framing is inspected in the rendered vertical file.

Sound: drums grab attention, speech carries the story, real transit ambience links the montage, and Leið music proves the product. Duck other sounds under the voice. Let the track play alone once rather than narrating over the entire example. No extra stock background track needed.

No narration synthesis for this founder story. ElevenLabs Ralf narration remains prepared for the separate loop/bass feature short.

## Founder recording checklist

- Vertical phone video showing the founder and enough of the drum kit to recognize the performance. Capture two or three brief fills with a little footage before/after each.
- Opening line looking toward the lens after the fill; final URL line to camera too. Separate takes of speech avoid cymbal spill. Natural speaking voice, no advert-announcer delivery.
- A clean full narration recording as well, with a little space between numbered sections. Separate clips are also fine. Keep raw audio/video without music/captions baked in.
- Three or four original Budapest transport shots, each recorded for several seconds so there is room to cut: tram passing, metro doors/platform, a bus/window view, route signage/map detail. The final edit uses only a few seconds of each as appropriate.
- Founder-made Budapest track and matching preset for the product section. Existing approved Budapest music is a usable creative starting point; choose the exact excerpt after recording and verify its matching UI view.

Once footage and voice arrive: inspect originals and duration; select the strongest fill and transit shots; capture the matching DAW views; assemble and caption; render a review MP4/cover and decode/inspect; founder listening/creative review before any publication.
