# Short: build around your loop

Updated November 13, 2026. Final video: `_videos/launch/out/your-loop-bass/leid-your-loop-bass-and-leads-final.mp4`. Uses the founder's screen recording, supplied before/after audio, and the same ElevenLabs narrator with a corrected Free-with-sign-up CTA. Nothing scheduled or published.

## Audience and promise

For producers who have a loop and want inspiration for what to add. Show Leið as a space to audition and shape musical parts around an uploaded loop. Present this specific example as founder-made; don't imply the parts are generated automatically.

## Recommended hook and script

**Hook:** “Got a loop? Build something around it.”

1. Got a loop? Build something around it.
2. Upload it to Leið and match the tempo.
3. I made a bass line and two leads for this loop.
4. Shape each part while your loop plays.
5. Sign up free and build around your own loop.

The final uses November 12 clips 1–4 and a November 13 replacement CTA in the same ElevenLabs voice/settings: Ralf Eisend - Deep and Gravely, Eleven v4, stability 0.19, similarity 0.75.

## Edit recommendation

Open with the dry loop, flash the finished sound immediately as a tease, then show the upload/tempo workflow and actual track-making interface. Reveal the finished version by bringing in the bass and two leads at their actual entrances. Keep labels synchronized with what is audible. End on the full arrangement and “Sign up free.” Aim for about 25–30 seconds, driven by the music rather than forced timestamps.

The supplied audio lengths differ (9.6 vs 19.2 sec), and their sample rates/bit depths differ. Listen to both and align a musically complete passage before editing; avoid suggesting they are equal-length exports. The supplied WAVs have not been auditioned in this session. Do not create solo stems from the stereo after mix or imply separate parts can be isolated unless the founder supplies stems or the Leið session confirms them.

## Product truth and CTA

CTA: **“Try it with your own loop”** → `https://layth.space/`.

The uploaded reference audio is stored in the user's browser IndexedDB. Shared preset links preserve track/song settings but do not carry the uploaded loop audio to another user's browser. If a preset-specific link is used, state that viewers should upload their own loop. The feature is Free with sign-up, as confirmed by the founder November 13. No automatic key matching or time stretching claims.
